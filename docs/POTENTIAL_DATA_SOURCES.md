## 3. EuroFIR — Membership Value Proposition

- **Org:** EuroFIR AISBL (non-profit, Brussels)
- **What membership unlocks beyond public national tables:**
  - **FoodEXplorer** — single-search across 36 national + specialized FCDBs
  - **FoodBasket** — recipe nutrient calc
  - **eBASIS** — bioactive compound + biological-effects db (see §4)
  - **ePlantLIBRA** — plant food supplements bioactives
  - **EuOpenFood / FoodCASE** software
- **Pulls in datasets harder to access directly:** Italy CREA, German BLS extensions, Bulgaria, Serbia, Greek tables, Slovak, Slovenian, Estonian Nutridata, Cyprus.
- **Cost:** ~€2-5k+/yr ordinary institutional; tiers for academic/individuals.

## High-Priority Shortlist for Compound-Coverage Gap Filling

If we want the highest-leverage additions next, in rough ROI order:

1. **NCC NDS-R / NCCDB (Univ. Minnesota)** — single most comprehensive U.S.-style merged db, 19k foods × 165 nutrients incl. flavonoids/PA. Used in NHANES NDS-R + most U.S. epi cohorts. **Paid academic license.**
2. **FAO/INFOODS BioFoodComp v4** — only global db of analytical-only data on biodiversity, indigenous foods, edible insects, fish, varieties. **Free.**
3. **EuroFIR FoodEXplorer membership** — single portal for ~36 national FCDBs incl. ones hard to scrape (Italy CREA, Greek tables, Slovak, Slovenian, Estonian, Bulgarian, Serbian, Cyprus). **Membership.**
4. **Souci-Fachmann-Kraut online** — German chemistry-grade reference. Often cited where BLS has gaps. **Paid.**
5. **FNDDS** — adds U.S. recipe disaggregation + dietary-survey-coded foods missing in FDC. **Free.**
6. **TBCA + TACO (Brazil)** — LATAM flagship, 3,400+ foods incl. biodiversity. **Free.**
7. **TürKomp** — Turkey, 520 foods × 100 components. Only modern Turkic-region table. **Free.**
8. **Sri Lanka FCDB, FCT Bangladesh, Pakistan FCT, Nepal DFTQC** — completes South Asia south of India. Free FAO PDFs.
9. **SPC Regional Pacific Nutrient DB 2020** — fills Oceania with 3,000+ harmonized items. Free.
10. **NCC + FNDDS Flavonoid db, USDA PA Release 2, USDA Choline Release 2, USDA Carotenoid 1998, Iowa State Isoflavone 2008** — free standalone PDFs, data not entirely merged into FDC.
11. **Monash FODMAP** — only lab-tested fructan/GOS/polyol db. **Paid.**
12. **Sydney Univ. International GI Database** — 4,000+ entries GI/GL ground truth. Free.
13. **PhytoHub + KNApSAcK + HMDB + FOBI** — not nutrient content per se, but cross-walks for compound IDs across food → metabolite → biomarker. Free.
14. **EFSA Comprehensive European Food Consumption DB + FAO/WHO GIFT** — dietary intake context for pattern engine. Free.
