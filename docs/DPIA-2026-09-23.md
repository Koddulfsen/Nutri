# Data Protection Impact Assessment — Nutri (alpha)

**Status: DRAFT — prepared by Claude Code, 2026-09-23, for Jens (controller) to review, edit, and adopt as an internal record before alpha processing begins.** This is not a substitute for legal advice. It follows the structure required by GDPR Article 35(7) and EDPB guidance (WP248), and is scoped to what the Nutri codebase actually does as of this date — not aspirational future features.

**Controller:** Jens (Nutri), Norway.
**Supervisory authority:** Datatilsynet, Postboks 458 Sentrum, 0105 Oslo, Norway. https://www.datatilsynet.no

---

## 1. Why a DPIA, and whether it's strictly mandatory

GDPR Article 35(3)(b) makes a DPIA mandatory for "processing on a large scale of special categories of data" (Article 9). Nutri processes one Article 9 field — `life_stage` (pregnant/lactating) — for personalized nutrient targets.

At alpha's actual scale (a handful of users, verified against the live database: 3 `user_profiles` rows as of this writing), "large scale" is genuinely arguable — Norway's Datatilsynet publishes a national list of processing types that *always* require a DPIA (implants/novel welfare tech, large-scale IoT/wellness-device collection, large-scale biometric ID, genetic data at scale, predictive analytics on health data at scale), and Nutri does not clearly land on that specific list at this size.

**This DPIA is being done as prudent practice ahead of scale, not because the letter of the mandatory threshold is unambiguously met today.** It should be revisited and reissued when the user base grows, or immediately if scope expands (e.g. wellness/symptom tracking, currently shelved, is reactivated — see `docs/DATA-SCOPE-DECISIONS.md`).

## 2. Systematic description of the processing

### 2.1 What is collected, and why

| Data | Purpose | Legal basis |
|---|---|---|
| Email + password (via Supabase Auth) | Account identity | Contract (Art. 6(1)(b)) — necessary to provide the service |
| Meal logs (food, date, portion) | Core tracking feature | Contract (Art. 6(1)(b)) |
| Birth year + month | Age-banded Daily Value personalization | Consent (Art. 6(1)(a)) — optional, feature degrades gracefully without it |
| Biological sex | Sex-specific Daily Value personalization | Consent (Art. 6(1)(a)) |
| `life_stage` (pregnant/lactating) | Pregnancy/lactation-adjusted Daily Values | **Explicit consent, Art. 9(2)(a)** — separate, specific toggle (`sensitiveHealthData`), never bundled into a general "Accept All" |
| Activity level (planned, not yet built) | Future DV personalization | Would be Consent (Art. 6(1)(a)) when built |
| Free-text chat messages (AI food logger) | Natural-language food logging | **Explicit consent, Art. 6(1)(a)** (`aiProcessing`) — required before any message is sent to the Anthropic API |
| Custom Daily Value overrides | User-set nutrient targets | Contract (Art. 6(1)(b)) |
| Consent flags themselves | Proving consent was given (Art. 7(1) accountability) | Legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c)) |
| IP address, user agent (audit log) | Security — detecting abuse, incident investigation | Legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)) — IPs are truncated/pseudonymized at write time (verified, `anonymizeIP`) |

**Not collected, deliberately:** full date of birth (year+month only — a full DOB was found to be an unused quasi-identifier and removed, see CLAUDE.md §2); symptom/wellness free text (feature shelved, tables kept inert — see `docs/DATA-SCOPE-DECISIONS.md`).

### 2.2 Who processes it (recipients / sub-processors)

| Recipient | Role | What they receive | Location | Transfer safeguard |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Supabase | Hosting (Postgres + Auth) | Everything above | eu-west-1 (Ireland) | None needed — EU-hosted |
| Anthropic | AI food-logging assistant | User's typed chat messages (only if `aiProcessing` consent given) | US | Standard Contractual Clauses via Anthropic's Commercial Terms/DPA. **Action for Jens: confirm the DPA is active on this specific Anthropic account/API tier** — publicly documented DPA terms exist, but per-account activation for a self-serve API key was not independently verifiable from outside the account. No data is sold. No data is used for marketing without separate `newsletter` consent. No profiling with legal/significant effect occurs (DV personalization adjusts *displayed numbers*, it does not make decisions about the person).

### 2.3 Data subjects

Adults and older teens (16+, enforced at signup — see age-gate rationale in `docs/DATA-SCOPE-DECISIONS.md`) who create a Nutri account. No children's data is knowingly collected.

### 2.4 Retention

Per the decision in `docs/DATA-SCOPE-DECISIONS.md`: indefinite while the account is active (no silent auto-expiry — verified, no TTL/cron job deletes user data), immediate and complete deletion on request (verified end-to-end — see CLAUDE.md task 2.8). Audit log rows are retained indefinitely at present with no defined retention policy or deletion job; **this is an open gap**, not a decision — see §5 below.

## 3. Necessity and proportionality assessment

- **Is each field necessary for its stated purpose?** Yes for meal logs and account identity (core function). For demographics, the app functions with defaults if the user withholds consent — nothing is gated behind providing them except the DV personalization feature itself, so the "necessity" bar is satisfied by making them genuinely optional, not required.
- **Is `life_stage` proportionate?** This is the one field carrying real Article 9 risk. It was kept deliberately (a documented product decision, not an oversight) because pregnancy/lactation materially changes recommended nutrient intake and the alternative (dropping it) would have avoided nearly all of this compliance burden. The proportionality argument rests on: (a) it's opt-in via its own explicit toggle, (b) it's encrypted at rest with the key held separately from the data, (c) it is never disclosed to any third party, (d) the app functions without it.
- **Is sending free text to Anthropic proportionate?** The feature (natural-language food logging) has real user value and a non-AI fallback (manual search) already exists in the app, so the AI path is not the only way to use Nutri. Proportionate given: opt-in consent, no training-data use per Anthropic's stated policy, ~30-day retention on Anthropic's side (not indefinite).
- **Could the purpose be achieved with less data?** Age was already reduced from full DOB to year+month after exactly this kind of review (§2). No further reduction identified for the fields currently collected.

## 4. Risk assessment

| Risk | Likelihood | Severity | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Database breach exposing `life_stage` in plaintext | Was High before 2026-09-23 | High if realized | **Mitigated**: `life_stage` is AES-256-GCM ciphertext; the key lives in a separate table (`user_encryption_keys`) from the data it protects, so a single-table compromise doesn't yield both. Verified via raw SQL read returning ciphertext. |
| One user's data leaking to another user (authorization bug) | Was unverified before 2026-09-23 | High if realized | **Mitigated**: full audit of all 27 files touching user-scoped tables completed 2026-09-23; every query verified to filter by the authenticated user's own id. See CLAUDE.md task 2.2. |
| User free text (potentially revealing health info, e.g. "I'm nauseous every morning") sent to Anthropic without the user realizing | Medium before consent gate | Medium | **Mitigated**: explicit, separate `aiProcessing` consent required before any message reaches the AI route; off by default; never bundled into "Accept All". |
| Re-identification via combining `birth_year`+`biological_sex`+`life_stage` | Medium, especially at low user counts during alpha | Medium | **Partially mitigated**: birth day already dropped (year+month only). `biological_sex` and `birth_year`/`birth_month` remain unencrypted — noted here and in `docs/DATA-SCOPE-DECISIONS.md` as a residual risk, not yet addressed. Small alpha user count increases re-identification risk per-record; revisit encrypting these fields as user count grows. |
| Deletion request not actually honored | Was near-certain before 2026-09-23 (endpoint was a stub) | High if realized | **Mitigated**: real, immediate deletion verified end-to-end against a live test account, including cascade to meals/consent/keys and deletion of the Supabase Auth credential itself. |
| Export request not actually honored | Was near-certain before 2026-09-23 | Medium | **Mitigated**: real synchronous export verified against a live account. |
| Audit log retained indefinitely | Certain (no retention policy exists) | Low-medium — audit log contains truncated/pseudonymized IPs and action metadata, not health content | **Not yet mitigated** — flagged as an open item, §5. |
| Anthropic (a US processor) receiving EU personal data | Certain, whenever `aiProcessing` consent is granted | Medium | **Partially mitigated** by SCCs in Anthropic's standard terms; **unverified** whether they're active on this specific account — action item for Jens. |
| No DPO appointed / no privacy policy published yet | Certain until published | Medium — this DPIA and the accompanying privacy policy draft are the remediation in progress | Track to completion before public alpha invites go out. |

## 5. Mitigations already implemented (as of 2026-09-23)

1. `life_stage` encrypted at rest, key stored separately (CLAUDE.md 2.5).
2. Full authorization audit of user-scoped queries, zero gaps found (CLAUDE.md 2.2).
3. Dedicated, specific Article 9 consent (`sensitiveHealthData`) and AI-processing consent (`aiProcessing`), both excluded from bulk "Accept All", both gating the actual code paths that use the data.
4. Every new user gets a real consent record at signup (previously, some users had none at all).
5. Real, immediate, verified account deletion and data export (CLAUDE.md 2.8).
6. 16+ age gate at the one point DOB is collected.
7. IP addresses truncated/pseudonymized before being written to the audit log.

## 6. Open items — not yet mitigated

1. **No retention/deletion policy for the audit log.** Recommend defining one (e.g. rolling deletion after N months) rather than indefinite accumulation, even though current audit rows are low-sensitivity.
2. **`biological_sex`/`birth_year`/`birth_month` are unencrypted.** Lower individual sensitivity than `life_stage`, but combine with it to narrow down individuals, especially while the user base is small. Revisit before or shortly after scale increases.
3. **Anthropic DPA activation on this specific account is unverified from outside the account** — Jens should confirm directly in the Anthropic console.
4. **Food-composition third-party APIs (USDA, CNF, etc.) have not been reviewed for their own data-handling terms** regarding the search terms Nutri sends them (CLAUDE.md 5.3, tracked separately).
5. **No privacy policy or Terms of Service is published yet** — companion document being drafted alongside this DPIA.
6. **No DPO has been appointed.** GDPR does not require one for an organization of this size and processing volume, but Jens is the de facto contact for data subject requests and complaints until stated otherwise in the privacy policy.

## 7. Conclusion

Given the mitigations in §5, the residual risk of Nutri's current processing is assessed as **low-to-medium**, concentrated in the open items in §6, none of which are currently blocking issues but all of which should be tracked to closure before scaling past alpha. **This assessment should be reviewed and reissued if**: wellness/symptom tracking is reactivated, the user base grows enough to clearly meet "large scale" under Article 35(3)(b), a new third-party processor is added, or any new Article 9-adjacent field is collected.
