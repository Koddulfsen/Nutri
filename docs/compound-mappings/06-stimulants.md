## Notes

- **Caffeine**: Safe limit ~400mg/day (FDA). Found in coffee, tea, chocolate, energy drinks
- **Theobromine**: Primary alkaloid in chocolate. ~10x less stimulating than caffeine
- **Theophylline**: Found in tea. Similar structure to caffeine, used medicinally for asthma
- These methylxanthines are likely only tracked by FDC, CNF, and FooDB (specialized databases)
