Chronic oral reference doses (RfD):

  Water exposure:  5 x 10^-4 (mg/kg-day)   critical effect: "Significant proteinuria"
  Food exposure:   1 x 10^-3 (mg/kg-day)   critical effect: "Significant proteinuria"

Both derived from a NOAEL with a composite uncertainty factor of 10, confidence rated high.
Last Updated: 10/01/1989.

Only the FOOD value is stored in Nutri: it is the one that applies to dietary exposure, which is
what this system measures. The water value is stricter and would be the wrong number to compare a
meal against.
