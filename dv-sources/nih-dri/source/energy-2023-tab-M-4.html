NOTE: cm = centimeter; d = day; in. = inch; lb = pound; kcal = kilocalorie; kg = kilogram; PAL = physical activity level; y = year.

a

For U.S. population, based on NHANES 2015–2018, estimated by age group.

b

Uses EER equations for adult women.

c

Age used to predict EER is based on specific age: 19–30 y: 25 y; 31–50 y: 40 y; 51–70 y: 60 y; > 70 y: 80 y; 19 y+: 50 y.

d

Inactive: 1.0 ≤ PAL < 1.53; low active: 1.53 ≤ PAL < 1.69; active: 1.69 ≤ PAL < 1.85; very active: 1.85 ≤ PAL < 2.5.

e

f
