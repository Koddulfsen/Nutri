Usage Guidelines
  • Primary (60-70%) — Main CTAs, high-emphasis actions
  • Secondary (20-25%) — Alternative actions, medium emphasis
  • Tertiary (10-15%) — Low-emphasis, ghost buttons
  • Quaternary (<5%) — Subtle actions, cancel/dismiss
  • Special (<5%) — Premium features, upgrades
